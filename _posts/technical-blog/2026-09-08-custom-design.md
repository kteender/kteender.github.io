**Brief**
Client lives in an area where the roads get very slick in the winter. He wanted a way to tally up the number of vehicles he has rescued from the ditch. He wanted the tally to evoke a WWII-era fighter nose art.

**Concept**
After reviewing a lot of WWII-era fighter plane nose art, I noticed that the planes tended to have a catchy name. I came up with 'Tow Angel', a play on 'snow angel that fit the winter theme of this project. Iperformed concept exploration on lightboard in greyscale.

The font was the last thing I figured out. I couldn't get it right. Then, I was out one night in downtown LA, and there was <a href="https://www.instagram.com/bowserzink/">a tattoo artist doing on-the-spot tattoos</a>. Looking at the lettering in his flash book, I realized a blackletter font would fit with the tough-looking angel perfectly

**Printing**
For this project, I handled finding a print vendor as well. I converted the graphic to a scalable vector image and sent it to the printer in three layers. I also stopped by <a href="https://signarama.com/locations/ca-inglewood">the printer in-person</a> to confirm that the layers I sent them would print well, and to see the print material in-person.

I then picked up the printed decals and mailed them to the client.
