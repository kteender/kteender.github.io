I make animation, write code for computer graphics software, and perform divination. I like projects that combine in-person interaction with computer-generated imagery. 

I'm based in LA. I grew up in central Illinois and graduated from Carnegie Mellon University.

Indigo Coyle is a stage name. I picked it when I began offering an intuitive entertainment service. The name is inspired by the title of Douglas Hofstadter's text on recursion and conciousness, *I Am a Strange Loop*. I go by Indigo in contexts where I do business as a fortune teller. 

I designed and coded this site myself. The url is inspired by my given name, Katie.
