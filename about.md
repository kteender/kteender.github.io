I make animation, write code for computer graphics software, and perform divination. I like projects that combine in-person interaction with computer-generated imagery. 

Indigo Coyle is a stage name. I picked it when I began offering an intuitive entertainment service. The name is inspired by the title of Douglas Hofstadter's text on recursion and conciousness, *I Am a Strange Loop*. To keep communication simple, I typically go by Indigo. 

I'm based in Los Angeles. I grew up in central Illinois and graduated from Carnegie Mellon University. Get in touch via <a href="mailto:contact@ktcg.art?subject=contact from KTCG ART">contact@ktcg.art</a>. I'm on <a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/katie-t-a5075a156/">LinkedIn</a>.

I designed and coded this site myself. The title is inspired by my given name, Katie.
